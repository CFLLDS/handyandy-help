# Quick answers

**I did not get the sign-in email.** Check your junk folder, and check you typed your work address. Links last 15 minutes, so ask for a fresh one if it has been longer.

**I can sign in but I see "No access yet".** Your account has no role. Ask the service coordinator to set one up.

**The form will not show a price.** Pick a job and enter the postcode. Both are needed.

**The form says "We don't take this on".** The job type is one the service does not offer, such as gas, fixed electrical, roofing or work at height. It cannot be booked.

**A visit shows "In Outlook, not a Handy Andy job yet".** It was booked in the Outlook diary. A coordinator can turn it into a Handy Andy job from the Bookings screen.

**I cannot edit a booking.** It has been paid or invoiced. Paid jobs need a refund first. Invoiced jobs need a credit note and a new invoice.

**My Day says the diary may be out of date.** The link to the Outlook diary has not refreshed for a while. Ring the office before relying on it.

**A customer wants to pay by card but the payment will not go through.** Do not write card details down. Tell the office, and they will send a payment link.

**I sent a payment link to the wrong person.** Tell finance straight away. They can replace the link, which stops the old one working.

**The VAT on an invoice is less than 20%.** That is expected. Energy saving measures are zero-rated, and some work for eligible customers has VAT relief. VAT is worked out per job.

**The Service screen says "Cannot say yet".** Enter what people are paid in the boxes at the top.

**The numbers on the Service screen do not match Invoicing.** The Service screen projects a recent period to a year, and its collected figures cover the whole ledger. Invoicing shows only what is waiting now.
