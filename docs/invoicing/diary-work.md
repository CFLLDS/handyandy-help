# Work finished in the diary

**Screen: Invoicing, "Finished in the diary, not yet invoiced"**

This panel lists completed visits that were only ever in the Outlook diary. The coordinator priced each one in the appointment notes, and the figures are shown exactly as written at the time.

The top of the panel shows how many jobs are waiting and their total value.

## Raise an invoice for one

1. Find the visit. Each line shows the date, what was done, the customer and the amount.
2. Select **Raise invoice**.
3. Under **Which job was it?**, choose the job type that matches the work.
4. Look at the VAT line. If the notes did not say, you will see **VAT, the form did not say**. Choose the correct treatment.
5. Select **Create booking and open invoice**.

Handy Andy creates a booking for the visit, so it has a proper record, and opens the invoice for you to check. From here it behaves like any other job. See [Invoice a finished job](invoice-a-job.md).

## What the VAT labels mean

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VAT due | The notes say VAT was charged |
| VAT exempt | The notes say no VAT applied |
| VAT not stated | The notes do not say. You must decide before invoicing |

If you are unsure which treatment applies, ask the coordinator what the job was and whether the customer's eligibility declaration was confirmed.
