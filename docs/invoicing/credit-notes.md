# Cancel an invoice

**Screen: Invoicing, on an invoiced job**

An issued invoice is never edited or deleted. If it was wrong, you cancel it with a credit note and issue a new one. That keeps the invoice numbers in an unbroken sequence, which is what an auditor expects to see.

## Raise a credit note

1. Find the invoiced job and select **Credit note**.
2. Your browser asks **Why is this invoice being cancelled?** Type the reason in plain words. For example: "Wrong VAT rate, declaration was on file".
3. Confirm.

You will see a message that the credit note cancels the invoice, with both numbers. Open the credit note to print or send it.

!!! warning "The reason is permanent"
    What you type is printed on the credit note and cannot be changed afterwards. Keep it factual, and do not include anything personal about the customer.

## Then put it right

The booking can now be corrected by the coordinator. When it is right, issue a new invoice in the usual way.

## If the customer has already paid

Refund the payment through the payment provider first, then raise the credit note and the new invoice.
