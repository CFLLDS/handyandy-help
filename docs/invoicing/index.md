# Invoicing

This section is for finance. The **Invoicing** screen lists finished work with the labour, travel, materials and VAT already worked out, so your job is to check, issue and collect.

## The page at a glance

**Three numbers at the top.**

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Awaiting invoice | Finished jobs that have not been invoiced yet |
| Value inc VAT | What those jobs are worth in total |
| Already paid | Jobs where the money has been received |

**Ready to invoice.** Everything below the numbers. It has three parts:

| Part | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Find a customer** | A search box. Type a name, a job or a postcode area |
| **Finished in the diary, not yet invoiced** | Work that was only ever in the Outlook diary. See [Work finished in the diary](diary-work.md) |
| The table | Handy Andy jobs that are done, with every figure broken out. See [Invoice a finished job](invoice-a-job.md) |

## Why some work is in a separate panel

The service still runs partly on the Outlook diary. A visit only reaches the main table once a coordinator has turned it into a Handy Andy booking. Until then, the price for a finished job lives in the appointment notes, exactly as the coordinator wrote it at the time. The separate panel makes sure that work is not forgotten.

## Finding the right job

Search results are listed newest first. Regular customers may have several jobs, so check the date before you invoice or take payment.
