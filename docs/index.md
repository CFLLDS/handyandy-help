# Handy Andy Help Centre

Guides for the people who run the handyperson service with Handy Andy: coordinators who take the bookings, the handyperson out on the road, finance, and the service manager.

Each guide is written around a job you need to do. Pick the one you need; most take two or three minutes to read.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Taking a booking**

    ---

    For coordinators. Price a job, get the VAT right, book it, send the confirmation, and change or cancel it later.

    [Start here](bookings/index.md)

-   **My Day**

    ---

    For the handyperson. Where to go, who to ring, what to know at the door, and how to finish a job and take payment.

    [Start here](my-day/index.md)

-   **Invoicing**

    ---

    For finance. Turn finished work into invoices, take card payments, and put right an invoice that was wrong.

    [Start here](invoicing/index.md)

-   **Running the service**

    ---

    For the service manager. Whether the service covers its costs, what has been collected, and what would change the picture.

    [Start here](service/index.md)

</div>

New to Handy Andy? Read [Getting started](getting-started/index.md) first. It takes five minutes.
