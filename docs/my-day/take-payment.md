# Take payment at the door

**Screen: My Day, on the job**

If the customer wants to pay by card when the job is done, you can take payment from your phone.

1. On the job, tap **Take card payment**.
2. Tap **Open SumUp payment page**. The payment page opens with the amount already filled in.
3. The customer completes the payment on that page.
4. Back in Handy Andy, tap **Check whether it has been paid**. The job shows the result.

## If the customer would rather pay on their own phone

Tap **Copy link**, then paste the link into a text or email to the customer. They can open it and pay without signing in to anything.

!!! warning "Treat the link with care"
    Anyone holding the link can see the household's details on the invoice. Send it only to the customer, and check the number before you send.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not paid yet, the page is still open | The payment was not completed. Try again, or check the card |
| Reopened the payment already started for this job | A payment was started earlier. You are taken back to it, so the customer is not charged twice |

If a payment will not go through, do not take card details down on paper. Tell the office, and they will send the customer an invoice with a link to pay.

Cash and bank transfer are recorded by the office, not in My Day.
