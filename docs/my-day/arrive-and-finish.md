# Arrive, finish and mark done

**Screen: My Day, on the job**

Recording when you arrive and finish gives the office an accurate time for the invoice, and gives you a record if a visit is ever questioned.

## When you get there

Tap **I have arrived**. The job shows **On site since** and the time.

## When the job is finished

Tap **I have finished**. The job shows how long you were on site.

## Mark the job done

1. Tap **Mark done**.
2. You are asked **How long did it take?** Enter the time if it was different from the estimate.
3. If the estimate was right, tap **Skip, just mark it done**.

The job moves to **Finished today** and appears on the finance screen, ready to invoice.

## If you forget

If you did not tap arrive or finish, you can still mark the job done and enter how long it took. The time is then shown as recorded by you, not from the taps.

## Jobs you cannot change

| The job shows | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Paid, cannot be edited** | The customer has paid. If something was wrong, the office has to refund it first |
| **Invoiced, cannot be edited** | An invoice has gone out. The office has to raise a credit note and a new invoice |

In both cases, ring the office. Do not try to fix it at the door.
