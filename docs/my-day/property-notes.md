# Note what you found

**Screen: My Day, on the job**

You will often learn something about a building that would save time next visit: what the walls are made of, where the stopcock is, that there is no loft hatch in the hall. Handy Andy keeps these notes **with the house, not the job**, so they are there for whoever visits next.

## Add or update a note

1. On the job, tap **Note what you found**. If there is already a note, the button says **Update what you found**.
2. Under **Walls**, choose what you found: Solid brick, Cavity, Timber frame, Stone or Plasterboard.
3. Under **Anything worth knowing next time**, add a short note. For example: "Stopcock under the stairs. No loft hatch in the hall."
4. Tap **Save**. You will see **Saved for next time**. If you have nothing to add, tap **Skip**.

Next time anyone visits that address, the note appears on the job as **Last visit found**.

!!! note "About the building only"
    These notes are about the property. Anything about how the customer is coping goes through [Raise a concern](raise-a-concern.md), so the right person sees it.
