# Raise a concern

**Screen: My Day, on the job**

You are often the only visitor a customer has that week. If something worries you, tell the office. It takes less than a minute, and you do not need to be sure.

!!! warning "In an emergency"
    If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 first. Then ring the office.

## Raise it

1. On the job, tap **Raise a concern**.
2. Under **What did you notice?**, choose the closest match:
    - Home condition or hazard
    - Health or mobility
    - Isolation or lack of support
    - Financial hardship or possible scam
    - Something else
3. Write **one line on what you saw**. For example: "Heating off, said the boiler broke in November." The counter shows how many characters you have left.
4. Tap **Send to coordinator**.

You will see **Passed to the coordinator**, and the job shows **Welfare concern raised**.

## What happens next

The coordinator sees the concern on the Bookings screen and decides what to do. You have done your part by telling them.

Write what you saw or heard, not what you think the cause is. Do not raise the concern in front of the customer unless they have asked you to.
