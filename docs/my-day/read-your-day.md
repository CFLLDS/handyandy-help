# Read your day

**Screen: My Day**

## Is it up to date?

Under **Your day** you will see when the diary was last updated. If it has not been updated for a while, a warning tells you the diary may be out of date. Check with the office before relying on it.

## Each day

Days are shown in date order, starting with today. On the right of each date is the number of jobs and the total time.

To look back, select **Show earlier days**.

### The map

Each day starts with a simple map of your stops, numbered in visit order, with the distance between them.

- Distances are **as the crow flies**, so the real drive will be longer.
- The map shows postcode areas, not house addresses.
- If no visit has a postcode on file, you will see **No map today**.

### The visits

Each visit shows:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| The number and time | Matches the number on the map. Underneath is how long the job is booked for |
| The job and the customer | What you are there to do, and for whom |
| **Directions** | Tap to open the address in your maps app |
| **Call** | Tap to ring the customer |
| The door notes | **Parking**, **Access**, **Pets** and **Key safe**, as the office recorded them |
| **Last visit found** | Notes about the building from a previous visit, if there are any |

### The gaps

Between visits you will see lines like **Free until 12:30, room for a job** or **too short to book**. The office sees the same gaps, so do not be surprised if a job appears in one.

## In Outlook, not a Handy Andy job yet

Some visits carry this label. They were booked in the Outlook diary and have not been brought into Handy Andy. You can see the details and go to the visit as normal, but there are no buttons to arrive, finish or take payment. If you need those, ask the office to turn it into a Handy Andy job.

## Finished today

Jobs you have marked done move to **Finished today** at the bottom of the screen.
