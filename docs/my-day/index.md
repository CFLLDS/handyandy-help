# My Day

This section is for the handyperson. **My Day** is built for a phone: it tells you where to go, who to ring and what to know at the door, and it takes one or two taps to record a job.

You do not price anything, decide any VAT or fill in any forms. That is done in the office before the job reaches you.

## The shape of your day

1. **Open My Day** and [read your day](read-your-day.md): the map, the visits and the gaps.
2. At each visit, tap **I have arrived**, do the job, then tap **I have finished**. See [Arrive, finish and mark done](arrive-and-finish.md).
3. If the customer is paying by card, [take payment at the door](take-payment.md).
4. If you learned something useful about the building, [note what you found](property-notes.md).
5. If you are worried about the customer, [raise a concern](raise-a-concern.md).
