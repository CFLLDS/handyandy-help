# The four screens

The bar at the top of every page shows the screens you can use. The one you are on is underlined.

## Bookings

The coordinator's screen. From top to bottom:

| Part | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Job & VAT advisor** | Describe a job in plain English and get a suggested job type and VAT rate |
| **New booking** | The form for pricing and booking a job |
| **Welfare concerns** | Concerns the handyperson has raised, waiting for you to act. Only shown when there are some |
| **In Outlook** | Appointments in the Outlook diary that are not Handy Andy jobs yet |
| **Calendar** | Every Handy Andy booking, with its status and price |

## My Day

The handyperson's screen. Each day has a small map of the stops, then the visits in time order with gaps shown between them. Finished work moves to **Finished today** at the bottom.

## Invoicing

The finance screen. Three numbers at the top (**Awaiting invoice**, **Value inc VAT**, **Already paid**), then **Ready to invoice** with a search box, the work finished in the Outlook diary, and the table of Handy Andy jobs.

## Service

The service manager's screen. It answers one question, **Is the service paying for itself?**, then shows what has been collected, what would change the position, and what the figures are built on.

## Statuses you will see

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | Booked and in the diary |
| Done | The handyperson has finished the job |
| Invoiced | An invoice has been issued. The figures are now fixed |
| Paid | Money has been received. The booking can no longer be edited |
| Cancelled | The booking was cancelled. Any late charge is shown next to it |
