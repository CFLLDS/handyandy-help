# Getting started

Handy Andy is the tool the handyperson service runs on. It prices a job from the rate card, works out the VAT, puts the visit in the diary, gives the handyperson a simple view of the day, and gives finance a list of finished work ready to invoice.

## Who uses what

Handy Andy has four screens. You see the ones that match your role.

| Screen | Who it is for | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| **Bookings** | Coordinators | Price and book jobs, send confirmations, change and cancel bookings, follow up welfare concerns |
| **My Day** | The handyperson | See the day's visits, get directions, record arrival and finish, take payment, raise a concern |
| **Invoicing** | Finance | Preview and issue invoices, take payment, raise credit notes |
| **Service** | The service manager | See whether the service covers its costs and what has been collected |

If you need a screen you cannot see, ask your service coordinator to change your role.

## Two diaries, one picture

The service has always run on a shared Outlook calendar, and it still does. Handy Andy reads that calendar, so visits booked in Outlook appear on screen next to visits booked in Handy Andy.

The link is one way. Handy Andy can read Outlook, but it does not change it.

An Outlook appointment shows the label **In Outlook, not a Handy Andy job yet**. You can see it and plan around it, but you cannot check in to it, invoice it or take payment for it until a coordinator turns it into a Handy Andy job. See [Turn an Outlook appointment into a job](../bookings/outlook-appointments.md).
