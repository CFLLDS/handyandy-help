# Sign in

There is no password to remember. You sign in with a link sent to your work email, or with your Microsoft work account.

## Sign in with an email link

1. Open Handy Andy in your browser.
2. Type **Your work email** and select **Email me a link**.
3. The screen changes to **Check your email**.
4. Open the email and select the link. You are signed in.

The link works once and expires after 15 minutes. If you typed the wrong address, select **Use a different address**.

## Sign in with Microsoft

Select the **Sign in with Microsoft** button and use your usual work account.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| That link has expired | More than 15 minutes have passed | A fresh link is sent automatically. Use the new email |
| That link has already been used | Each link works once | Ask for a new one |
| That link is not valid | The link was cut short, often by an email program | Ask for a new one and select it directly from the email |
| This address does not have a Handy Andy account yet | You signed in, but nobody has added you | Ask the service coordinator to add you |
| No access yet | You have an account but no role | Ask the service coordinator to set one up |
| No connection | Your device is offline | Check your signal or Wi-Fi and try again |

## Sign out

Select **Sign out** at the top right. Always sign out on a shared computer.
