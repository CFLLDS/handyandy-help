# Cancel a booking

**Screen: Bookings, Calendar**

1. Find the booking in the **Calendar**.
2. Select **Cancel booking**.
3. Read the message that appears. It tells you what was decided about any late charge and deposit, so you can tell the customer there and then.

The booking stays in the Calendar, marked **Cancelled**, with the outcome shown next to it.

!!! warning "Cancelling takes effect straight away"
    There is no second step asking whether you are sure, and any late charge is applied at that moment. Check you have the right booking before you select **Cancel booking**.

## Late cancellations and deposits

Handy Andy applies the service's cancellation rules for you and records the result on the booking.

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice | A late cancellation charge is added. The booking shows the amount |
| Cancelled in good time | The booking shows **No late charge** |
| Materials had already been bought | The deposit is kept to cover them, whether or not the cancellation was late |
| No materials had been bought | The deposit is refunded |

The two rules are separate. A customer who cancels in good time is not charged the late fee, even if the deposit is kept for parts already bought.

The outcome is worked out once, at the moment you cancel, and does not change afterwards.

## Bookings you cannot cancel

A job that is already done, invoiced or cancelled cannot be cancelled. If a finished job should not be charged, speak to finance.
