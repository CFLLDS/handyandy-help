# Turn an Outlook appointment into a job

**Screen: Bookings, In Outlook**

The **In Outlook** panel lists appointments in the handyperson's Outlook diary that are not Handy Andy jobs yet. They are read-only here, because the calendar link is one way.

While an appointment stays in Outlook only, the handyperson can see it in My Day but cannot check in to it, and finance cannot invoice it or take payment for it. Turning it into a Handy Andy job makes all three possible.

## Make it a Handy Andy job

1. Find the appointment in the **In Outlook** panel.
2. Select **Make this a Handy Andy job**.
3. Under **What is the job?**, choose the job type. The usual time for that job is shown next to it.
4. Select **Book it**. To back out, select **Cancel**.

The price comes from the rate card and this household's VAT position. You will see a message confirming the booking and the VAT rate, with any warnings, for example a missing postcode.

The Outlook appointment itself is not changed or removed.

## Work that is already finished

If the visit has already happened and was only ever in Outlook, finance can raise the invoice from the Invoicing screen. See [Work finished in the diary](../invoicing/diary-work.md).
