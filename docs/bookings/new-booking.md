# Take a new booking

**Screen: Bookings, New booking**

## 1. Choose the job

Open the **Job** list. Each job shows its usual time and its VAT treatment, for example "45m · relief". Pick the closest match.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| esm | An energy saving measure. Zero-rated for every customer |
| relief | VAT relief may apply. It depends on the household's eligibility |
| standard | VAT always applies |
| not offered | The service does not take this work on |

## 2. Who and where

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Customer name** | As the customer gives it |
| **Mobile** | The only number a reminder can be texted to. Ask for it even if they prefer the landline |
| **Landline** | Optional |
| **Postcode** | Needed for the travel charge and the map. Enter it before anything else if you want to see a price |
| **Email** | Optional. Useful for the confirmation and the invoice |
| **Address** | The link next to it opens the address in Google Maps so you can check it |

## 3. Hourly rate

Choose **Standard** or **Concession**. The amount is shown next to each. Follow your service's own policy on who qualifies for the concession rate.

## 4. Funding and eligibility

These two boxes decide the VAT and who pays. They matter enough to have their own page: [VAT, funding and eligibility](vat-funding-eligibility.md).

## 5. At the door

Four short notes that are **kept on the household**, so you only ask once:

- **Parking**, for example "On the drive" or "Road opposite"
- **Access**, for example "Side gate, ring twice"
- **Pets**, for example "Large dog, friendly"
- **Key safe**, for example "By the back door"

The handyperson sees these on the job in My Day. Record where a key safe is, never its code.

## 6. Materials

If the service is supplying parts, add them now or later. See [Materials and parts](materials.md).

## 7. Check the price

The quote builds up at the bottom of the form: **Labour**, **Materials (at cost)**, travel, **VAT** and **Total inc VAT**. If a deposit is needed, it says how much is due at booking. If the job is funded by an energy award, it says there is nothing to collect.

Read the total to the customer before you go on.

## 8. Book it

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Propose date** | Suggests the next sensible slot in the diary for a job of this length, grouping visits in the same area where it can |
| **Confirm booking** | Books the job. You see a message with the job, the customer and the time |

After booking, [send the confirmation](confirmation.md).

!!! tip "Pick a job and enter a postcode first"
    If you see this message, the form cannot price the job yet. Both are needed before a quote appears.
