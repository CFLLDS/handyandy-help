# Welfare concerns

**Screen: Bookings, Welfare concerns**

The handyperson is often the only visitor a customer has that week. If they notice something worrying, they can raise a concern from the job, and it appears here for the coordinator.

The panel only appears when there is at least one concern waiting.

## What you see

Each concern shows what kind it is, one line on what was noticed, the job it relates to and who raised it.

| Kind of concern |
|---|
| Home condition or hazard |
| Health or mobility |
| Isolation or lack of support |
| Financial hardship or possible scam |
| Something else |

## What to do

1. Read the concern and decide what it needs, following your organisation's own procedures. That might be a phone call, a referral to another service, or a safeguarding report.
2. Do it.
3. Select **Mark as actioned**. The concern leaves the list.

!!! warning "This is not an emergency route"
    If someone is in immediate danger, call 999. If you believe an adult is at risk of abuse or neglect, follow your safeguarding policy and speak to your safeguarding lead. Marking a concern as actioned in Handy Andy is a record that you acted; it does not tell anyone else.
