# Change a booking

**Screen: Bookings, Calendar**

## Move a visit or change its notes

1. Find the booking in the **Calendar** and select **Edit**.
2. Change what you need to:
    - **Starts** and **Ends**
    - **What is different about this visit**, for example "Side gate will be unlocked on the day"
    - **Job notes**
3. Select **Save changes**. To leave it alone, select **Stop editing** or **Close**.

If you change the length of the visit, the labour is priced again and the message tells you the new time and labour charge. Travel and VAT stay as agreed, because those were decided for the household, not for the date.

After moving a visit, [send the confirmation](confirmation.md) again so the customer has the new time in writing.

## Bookings you cannot edit

| The booking shows | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid, cannot be edited** | Somebody handed over money against these figures | Refund through the payment provider first, then correct the booking |
| **Invoiced, cannot be edited** | The invoice holds its own copy of the figures | Raise a credit note and a new invoice. See [Cancel an invoice](../invoicing/credit-notes.md) |

## Mark a job done from the office

If the handyperson has finished a job but has not marked it, select **Done** on the booking. It moves on to finance as finished work, ready to invoice.
