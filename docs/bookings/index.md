# Taking a booking

This section is for coordinators. It covers the **Bookings** screen: pricing a job, getting the VAT right, putting it in the diary and looking after it until the day.

## The shape of a booking

1. **Listen to what the customer wants.** If you are unsure which job type fits, or what VAT applies, [ask the advisor](job-and-vat-advisor.md).
2. **Fill in the booking form.** The price builds up on screen as you go. See [Take a new booking](new-booking.md).
3. **Tell the customer the price** before you confirm. Nothing on the quote should come as a surprise on the day.
4. **Confirm the booking** and [send the confirmation](confirmation.md).
5. **Look after it.** [Change](change-a-booking.md) or [cancel](cancel-a-booking.md) it if plans move.

## What the price is made of

| Part | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Labour | The job's estimated time, charged at the hourly rate you choose |
| Travel | A flat charge, banded by the customer's postcode |
| Materials | Parts the service supplies, charged at cost |
| VAT | Worked out for this job and this household. See [VAT, funding and eligibility](vat-funding-eligibility.md) |
| Deposit | Asked for on larger jobs. The form tells you when one is due and how much |

Time is charged in 30-minute blocks. A job estimated at 40 minutes is billed as an hour, and the form shows this so you can explain it.

## Jobs the service does not take on

Some job types appear in the list marked **not offered**, such as fixed electrical work, gas work, work at height, roofing and structural work. If you pick one, the form shows **We don't take this on** and will not let you book it. Suggest the customer looks for a qualified trade instead.
