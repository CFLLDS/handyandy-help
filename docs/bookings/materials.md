# Materials and parts

**Screen: Bookings, in the booking form, or the Materials button on a booking in the Calendar**

Materials the service supplies are charged to the customer at cost. Adding them early matters, because materials can trigger a deposit.

## Let Handy Andy work out the parts

1. In **What did they ask for?**, type the request in the customer's words. For example: "Put up two curtain rails that have fallen down, and hang a mirror in the hall".
2. Select **Work out the parts for this job**.
3. Handy Andy reads the note and lists the parts. You will see up to three groups:
    - **Parts the service supplies**, added to the quote at cost
    - **Not mentioned, but this job usually needs**, worth checking before you quote
    - **Usually bought by the household**, so check they have these in before the visit
4. Remove anything that does not apply.

If there is no standard parts list for the job, you are asked to describe the job or add materials by hand.

## Add or remove a part by hand

1. On the booking in the **Calendar**, select **Materials**.
2. Type what the part is, for example "grab rail kit", and its cost.
3. Select **Add**. To take one off, select **Remove** next to it.
4. Select **Hide materials** when you have finished.

The total and any deposit are worked out again each time materials change.

!!! note "Once a job is paid or invoiced"
    Materials cannot be changed on a booking that has been paid or invoiced. See [Change a booking](change-a-booking.md) for what to do instead.
