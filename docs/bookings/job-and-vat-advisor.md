# Ask the job and VAT advisor

**Screen: Bookings, at the top**

The advisor helps you pick the right job type and understand the VAT before you quote. Use it whenever a request does not obviously match a job on the list.

## Ask a question

1. In the **Job & VAT advisor** box, describe the job and the customer in plain English. For example: "fit a grab rail for a disabled lady, declaration signed".
2. Select **Ask**.
3. Read the answer. It suggests a job type and a VAT rate, and gives the reason.
4. If it fits, select **Use this job type**. The booking form below is filled in with that job.
5. Check the eligibility boxes and the postcode on the form. The advisor fills in the job, not the household.

Select **Hide** to fold the advisor away when you do not need it.

## What it is and is not

!!! warning "Guidance, not tax advice"
    The advisor's answers are illustrative. They help you choose; they are not a ruling. If a case is unusual, check it against HMRC Notice 701/7 or ask finance before you invoice.

Do not type anything into the advisor that you would not write on the booking itself. Describe the job and the customer's circumstances; leave out names and addresses.

If the advisor shows **The assistant is unavailable right now**, carry on with the form. The job list still shows the VAT treatment of every job.
