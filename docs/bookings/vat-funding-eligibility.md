# VAT, funding and eligibility

**Screen: Bookings, New booking**

Handy Andy works out the VAT for each job, so you do not have to. Your part is to record the facts about the household accurately, because the VAT follows from them.

## Three kinds of job

| Kind of job | What the form says | VAT |
|---|---|---|
| Energy saving measure | "This is an energy measure. It is zero-rated VAT for every customer" | Zero-rated, whatever the customer's age or tenure. No declaration is needed |
| Relief-eligible job | "Relief-eligible job. VAT depends on the declaration above" | Reduced or zero-rated if the household qualifies and the declaration is confirmed. Otherwise standard |
| Standard-rated job | "Standard-rated job. VAT always applies" | Standard rate |

## Eligibility, captured once

Under **Eligibility**, tick what applies:

- **Customer is disabled**
- **Aged 60 or over**
- **Their own home**
- **Eligibility declaration confirmed**

These are kept on the household, so you do not ask again at the next booking. Only tick **Eligibility declaration confirmed** when the customer has actually made the declaration. It is the evidence behind the VAT rate on the invoice, and finance may be asked to show it.

## Funding

For an energy measure you will see a **Funding** box.

Tick **Paid by an energy redress award** when the work is being paid for by the award and not by the household. Add the **Award reference** if you have it.

When this is ticked:

- the household is not invoiced
- no deposit is asked for
- the job is still costed in full, because the award is reported against these figures

## If you are not sure

[Ask the advisor](job-and-vat-advisor.md), and if it is still unclear, check with finance before you confirm the price to the customer. It is much easier to get VAT right at booking than to correct an invoice afterwards.
