# Running the service

This section is for the service manager. The **Service** screen answers one question: is the handyperson service paying for itself?

It is measured from the live handyperson diary, not from a forecast. You give it four figures about what things cost, and everything else on the page is worked out from what actually happened.

| Part of the page | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| [Is the service paying for itself?](paying-for-itself.md) | Whether income covers wages and overheads, and by how much |
| [What has actually been collected](collected.md) | Money received, and delivered work that has not been paid for |
| [What would change it](what-would-change-it.md) | What each option would be worth in a year |
| What this is built on | The period measured, days worked, visits and hours, so you can judge how far to trust the answer |

!!! note "A projection and a ledger, side by side"
    The first panel is a projection: it takes a recent window and scales it to a year. The second is receipts: money that has actually arrived. They answer different questions and will not match.
