# What would change it

**Screen: Service, third panel**

This panel prices the options open to you. Each one shows what it would be worth in a year **on its own**, at today's productivity, not at a hoped-for one.

Typical options:

| Option | The question it answers |
|---|---|
| A further operating day | What if the handyperson worked one more day a week? |
| One more visit per day | What if the diary was packed a little tighter? |
| A rate rise | What if the hourly charge went up? |
| Give admin time back | What if bookings and invoicing took less office time? |
| Charge for quoting visits | What if free quoting visits were charged for? |

The bars let you compare the options at a glance.

## Not yet priced

An option marked **Not yet priced** needs a figure that has not been entered, usually what people are paid. Fill in the boxes at the top of the page and it will be worked out.

## Reading it sensibly

- The options are priced one at a time. Two together will not be worth exactly the sum of both.
- A change that looks small per visit can be the largest over a year, because it applies to every visit.
- These are estimates to support a decision. Take the figures to your finance lead before you change a price or a contract.
